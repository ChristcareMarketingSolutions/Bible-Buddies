Free Sunday school lessons for kids

Each story below is a ready-to-use free Sunday school lesson for kids aged 4 to 11, with the Bible story, a discussion question, a memory verse idea, a game or coloring activity, a take-home challenge and a closing prayer. Use them for Sunday school, children's church, VBS (Vacation Bible School), kids' clubs or homeschool Bible lessons. Pair them with our Bible games, memory verses and Bible coloring pages.

  14. Three Friends in the Fiery Furnace (Daniel 3)
  15. Daniel in the Lions' Den (Daniel 6)
  16. Jonah and the Big Fish (Jonah 1–3)
  17. Jesus Is Born (Luke 1–2)
  18. The Wise Men Follow the Star (Matthew 2)
  19. Jesus as a Boy in the Temple (Luke 2:41–52)
  20. Jesus Calls the Fishermen (Luke 5:1–11)
  21. Jesus Turns Water into Wine (John 2:1–11)
  22. Jesus Calms the Storm (Mark 4:35–41)
  23. Jesus Feeds 5,000 People (John 6:1–14)
  24. Jesus Walks on the Water (Matthew 14:22–33)
