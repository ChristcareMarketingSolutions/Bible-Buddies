A virtual tour of the Bible lands for kids

The Bible Buddies 3D walk is like a street view of Jesus' life: children press Forward and walk a cartoon road through the Holy Land, stopping at 40 places from the Gospels and Acts. At each stop they read what happened there, straight from the Bible, with the Bible reference and a key verse. Visit Bethlehem where Jesus was born, Nazareth where He grew up, the Jordan River where He was baptized, the Sea of Galilee where He calmed the storm, Jerusalem, the Garden of Gethsemane, Golgotha and the empty tomb. It is free, safe, works in the browser and is great for Sunday school, homeschool and Easter or Christmas lessons. Read more in our Bible stories for kids.
